| D-23 | 2026-11-12 | One person per browser (owner: fix the sign-in bug). Found on the demo: "Sign out" on core's home page ended only the core screens' session; the products' single sign-on stayed, so after another person signed in on core a product still opened as the previous person, and core's sign-in did not sign anyone on to the products although the page said so. Now (1) core's sign-in starts the single sign-on for that person (the provider's own session and signed cookie), keeping it if it is the same person and otherwise first ending the previous one; (2) core's sign-out always works, also after the screens' session went idle, and ends the single sign-on as the provider's "sign out" does: each product it signed into is told by OIDC Back-Channel Logout 1.0, their grants and refresh tokens are revoked, the session is deleted; (3) "Yes, sign me out" on core's sign-out page (a product's logout) also ends the core screens' session; (4) signed in through a product, the core screens open as that person. A product opts in to back-channel logout with `signon.backchannel_logout` (a path) in its manifest; apply passes `backchannel_logout_uri` to core. A product without it still loses the person at its next renewal (at most 5 minutes, revoked grant). Tests: `test/signon.test.ts` (PL-01 one person per browser). |
