# Demo script: Medhanta TIDE

Feature walkthrough for **Medhanta TIDE** (recruitment with online tests and live quizzes, formerly "Recruitment"), about 5 min 44 s. One working day of hiring at the fictional **Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2**: the QC lab needs two **QC Analysts** and Production needs four **Production Operators**. A requisition is approved, candidates apply, QC candidates take an online test, Production candidates take a live quiz in the room (projector and phones side by side), the best go to interviews, and one QC Analyst is offered the job, checked against the GMP requirements and hired. The same day, an operator's SOP training is recorded and signed, for TrueBatch to read. A candidate also uses her data rights.

Cast (all fictional): Kavita Menon (HR Manager, also the contact person for personal data), Arjun Pillai (HR), Dr. Anita Desai (QC Manager), Vikram Shah (Production Head and trainer), Asha Rao (QA Head, interviewer and training approver), Neha Iyer (Engineering), Rahul Mehta (Warehouse), Suresh Kulkarni (site admin); operators Priya Nair and Deepak Joshi. Candidates: Ananya Kulkarni, Rohit Deshmukh, Sneha Patil, Farhan Qureshi, Meghna Joshi, Karan Bhatia (QC Analyst); Suresh Pawar, Imran Shaikh, Deepa Salunkhe, Pooja Shinde, Kiran Jadhav, Manoj Gaikwad, Ravi Chavan, Lata More (Production Operator). SOP numbers and titles are fictional. All names, emails, phone numbers, employers and addresses are fictional demo data.

Plain-word glossary for the voice-over: **requisition** – request to hire · **GMP** – pharma quality rules · **competent technical staff** – qualified lab staff · **shortlist** – next-round list · **cut-off** – pass mark · **SOP** – standard operating procedure · **QMS** – quality system (Medhanta ASSURE) · **e-signature** – signing with your password · **TrueBatch** – Medhanta's electronic batch record · **DPDP Act** – India's data protection law · **hand-over record** – file for personnel and training.

Screen positions are rough, on the 1920×1080 screenshot. The app was captured at 125 % zoom; the content column runs roughly from x 40 to x 1880; the menu bar with the Medhanta TIDE emblem is the top 50 px. Public candidate pages (07, 08, 14, 15, 44) use a centred column and carry the company name, as candidates see them. Screens 21, 23 and 24 put the projector (left) and a candidate's phone (right) side by side. Screens 37 and 48 are PDF pages on a grey background. Times in the app show as India time; the voice-over does not mention clock times.
