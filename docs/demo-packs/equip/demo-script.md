# Demo script: Medhanta EQUIP

Feature walkthrough, about 5 min 24 s. One working day at the fictional **Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2**: the new fluid bed dryer **FBD-01** goes from user requirements to *Qualified*, then a look at how EQUIP connects to other Medhanta products.
Cast (all fictional): Neha Iyer (Engineering, protocol author), Vikram Shah (Production, runs the tests), Priya Nair (Validation, second-person reviewer), Asha Rao (QA Head, approves), Rahul Mehta (QC), Suresh Yadav and Kavita Menon (aseptic operators). Products: Paracetamol 500 mg, Ibuprofen 400 mg and Metformin 500 mg tablets (DEMO); batches DEMO-24-001 to DEMO-24-003.

Plain-word glossary for the voice-over: **URS** – what it must do · **IQ** – installed as designed · **OQ** – works across ranges · **PQ** – performs in real use · **discrepancy** – a recorded failure · **e-signature** – password asked again · **media fill** – aseptic filling test · **MACO** – safe carry-over limit.

Screen positions are rough, on the 1920×1080 screenshot (captured at 125 % zoom; content sits in a centred column, roughly x 190–1730). Sign-in is through the Medhanta platform; for these screenshots the app ran with the same platform sign-in stand-in that its own browser tests use. Times in the app show as evening IST; the voice-over does not mention clock times.

## Intro (≈ 14 s)

**Problem in one line:** Qualifying equipment on paper means thick binders, lost signatures and missed review dates.

**Intro title card:** **medhanta-equip-logo.png** (the Medhanta EQUIP emblem) large and centred · title "Medhanta EQUIP" · subtitle "Equipment qualification and validation protocols, on screen" · small line "by Medhanta" with medhanta-logo.png · gradient underline.

**Voice-over:** "Qualifying a new machine means a URS, IQ, OQ and PQ: paper binders, wet signatures, failed steps to track and review dates to remember. Let's follow one new dryer at our demo plant from requirements to Qualified."
