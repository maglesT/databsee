# Demo script: Medhanta CLEAR

Feature walkthrough, about 5 min 26 s. One working day for QA at the fictional **Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2**, an oral-solids plant that exports: Asha Rao (QA Head) builds a GMP audit checklist for India, EU and US export from the regulation library, runs a warehouse self-inspection with Kiran Desai (QA Officer), records two findings, reports them, and Dr. Meera Joshi (Plant Head) closes the audit.
Cast (all fictional): Asha Rao (QA Head, lead auditor), Kiran Desai (QA Officer, auditor), Dr. Meera Joshi (Plant Head), Rahul Mehta (Warehouse) and Vikram Shah (Production) as auditees, Priya Nair (Documentation) and Sanjay Kulkarni as auditees of earlier audits. Rooms, equipment, SOP numbers, observations and dates are fictional demo data. Regulation text on screen is what the app itself shows; the voice-over only paraphrases.

What kind of tool: Medhanta CLEAR is a web app (runs in the browser on PC or tablet), a product of the Medhanta platform: one Medhanta sign-in, people picked from Medhanta's employee list, rooms / equipment / products from Medhanta master data. For this demo the Medhanta core sign-in and master data were stand-ins with fictional data, set up the way the product's own platform tests do it.

Name: always **"Medhanta CLEAR"** (said "meh-DHAAN-tah clear"), on every title card and every caption card. Logo: the CLEAR emblem logo (brand/medhanta-clear-logo.png, from the product's own brand folder).

Plain-word glossary for the voice-over: **self-inspection** – internal audit · **finding** – something not compliant · **grade** – how serious (critical, major, other) · **auditee** – person being audited · **SOP** – written procedure · **Schedule M** – India's GMP rules · **EU GMP** – Europe's GMP guide · **21 CFR** – US GMP rules.

Screen positions are rough, on the 1920×1080 screenshot (the app was captured at 150 % zoom; content sits in a centred column, roughly x 150–1770; the menu bar is the top 70 px). Times in the app show as night-time IST; the voice-over does not mention clock times. Every caption is shown on a caption card tagged "Medhanta CLEAR".

**Problem in one line:** Self-inspection checklists often miss what inspectors look for, and every market has its own GMP text.

**Intro title card:** Medhanta CLEAR emblem logo · "Medhanta CLEAR" · subtitle "Compliance Library for Evaluation and Audit Readiness" · small "by Medhanta" line · gradient underline.

**Voice-over:** "Self-inspection checklists often miss what inspectors look for. Medhanta CLEAR builds your GMP audit checklist from India's Revised Schedule M, EU GMP and US 21 CFR, and runs the audit on screen."
**Caption:** Medhanta CLEAR: audit checklists from the GMP rules
