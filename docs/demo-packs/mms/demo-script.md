# Medhanta MMS – demo video script

Feature walkthrough, 16:9, 1920×1080. Estimated length **4:57** (49 screens). Story: one working day at the fictional *Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2*. Medhanta MMS = Material Management System (said "em-em-ess").

People (all fictional): Asha Rao – QA Head; Meera Joshi – QA Officer; Kavya Patel – QC Analyst; Rahul Mehta – Stores / Warehouse; Vikram Shah – Production; Priya Nair – Production Operator; Neha Iyer – Purchase; Karan Desai – Site Admin.

Products (fictional demo data): TrueBatch Tablets makes Chlorphenamine Maleate Tablets 4 mg, sugar-coated (FG-CPM4); TrueBatch Injectables makes Ceftriaxone for Injection 1 g (FG-CFX1); Paracetamol Tablets 500 mg (FG-PCM500) is still made on a paper batch record, so its material is issued from Medhanta MMS.

Positions in 'Highlight' are on the 1920×1080 screenshot (top bar = app navigation). Every caption starts with "Medhanta MMS ·". Chapter durations include the chapter title card (~2.5 s).

## Intro

- **Problem (one line):** Paper logs and spreadsheets cannot prove which material lot went into which batch, who checked it, and when.
- **Intro title card:** Medhanta MMS emblem logo · "Medhanta MMS" · subtitle "Material management – from supplier to shipment" · gradient underline. (~4 s)
