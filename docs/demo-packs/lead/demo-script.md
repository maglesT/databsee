# Demo script: Medhanta LEAD

**Medhanta LEAD** (Logbook and Engineering Work Order Management), formerly "Pharma Logbook". One working day at the
fictional **Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2**. All people, products, batches and numbers are fictional demo data.

People in the story: Anil Kumar (Site Admin), Priya Nair (QA), Rakesh Patil (Production supervisor), Suresh Yadav
(Operator), Imran Shaikh (Engineer), Neha Iyer (Engineering Head).

Screen positions: "top-left", "centre", etc. refer to the 1920×1080 screenshot. Captions are at most about 8 words.
Every screen header shows the Medhanta LEAD mark and name.

Planned length: **≈ 5 min 44 s** (see Timing at the end).

---

## Intro (≈ 12 s)

**Title card:** Medhanta LEAD emblem · "Medhanta LEAD" · "Logbook and Engineering Work Order Management"

**Problem in one line:** Paper logbooks and work-order slips get missed, signed late and are hard to review.

**Voice-over:** "In a pharma plant every machine has a logbook: cleaning, use, calibration, breakdowns, and the
engineering work that fixes them. On paper these get missed. Let's spend one day at our demo plant with Medhanta LEAD."

**Caption:** Medhanta LEAD: logbooks and work orders
