# TrueBatch Injectables: demo video script

**Product:** TrueBatch Injectables (electronic batch manufacturing record for sterile injectables), with TrueBatch
Injectables Packing, by Medhanta
**Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, calm step-by-step tutorial, captions on. **Target length:** about 5 min 50 s (3–6 min).
**Story:** one working day at the fictional *Medhanta Demo Plant, Unit 2*. A master recipe for *Ceftriaxone 1 g powder
for injection* (product FG-CFX1, a sterile powder filled aseptically into vials) is built, checked and approved. Batch
TI260019 is dispensed, its stoppers siliconised and sterilised, the powder filled under grade A, inspected, reviewed by
exception and released; batch TI260020 meets a lot with too little stock at dispensing; TI260021 is issued. TI260019 is
then labelled and cartoned as packing orders TIP260006 and TIP260007. All people, products, lots and numbers on screen
are fictional demo data.

**Cast (fictional, as shown on screen):** Kavya Reddy – process author (writes the master) · Vikram Shah – production
manager (checks the master, issues batches) · Asha Rao – QA head (approves, decides exceptions, releases) · Meera Iyer –
QA officer (makes the master effective) · Rahul Mehta – dispensing operator · Arjun Das – washing and sterilisation
operator · Priya Nair – aseptic operator · Imran Khan – second-person checker · Deepa Menon – visual inspector · Sanjay
Kulkarni – production supervisor · Lakshmi Iyengar – QC microbiology · Ravi Kumar – packing operator.

**Notes for the editor**
- Every screen carries the app's own red banner "DEMO · a demonstration installation, not for GMP use · waits can be
  skipped". It is real; keep it. In the demo, the 30-minute rounds (fill weight, EM, sealing, tunnel) were skipped with
  the app's "Skip wait (demo)" so the day fits in the video; the record lists each skip for QA. For the same reason the
  intervention and the EM plate times on some screens read a few minutes or "0 min": do not zoom on them.
- Medhanta MMS (Material Management) is not connected in this demo installation. Its lots, stock and certificate values
  (assay and water) come from a stand-in made the way the product's own tests make it (lots CFX-S-2611 with 42.5 kg and
  CFX-S-2612 with 26.0 kg of sterile ceftriaxone sodium, vials, stoppers, seals, labels, cartons, leaflets). The app reads
  it exactly as it reads MMS. Medhanta LEAD (Logbook), DIVE (Deviations), QLIMS, EQUIP and TIDE are not linked: the app
  asks for those values by hand, a second person checks them, and QA sees them listed as "typed by hand".
- The batch numbers TI260019–TI260021 and the packing orders TIP260006–TIP260007 were given by the app's own counter
  (it continued from 18 earlier batches and 5 earlier packing orders this year, as on the owner's demo platform).
- Ceftriaxone is a sterile powder fill: it has no freeze-drying, no sterile filtration of a solution and no terminal
  sterilisation. Those features are shown in chapter 3 on two other draft masters of the same plant (FG-PAN40
  pantoprazole for injection, freeze-dried; FG-OND2 ondansetron injection, terminally sterilised). Their master check
  panels are red because they are drafts still being written: do not zoom on them.
- Positions are rough, on the 1920×1080 screenshot. The app's content sits in the middle of the screen
  (about x 360–1560).
